Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

First Connections ("we", "us", "our") is the North East's umbrella business networking platform, connecting Darlington First, Newcastle First, the NE High Street Heroes Awards and First Founders. This policy explains what personal data we collect through first-connections.co.uk, why we collect it, and how we look after it.

What we collect

We only collect what you give us directly, through forms on this site:

  • Contact form: first name, last name, email, phone (optional), company (optional), subject and message.
  • Newsletter sign-up: first name, last name, email, phone (optional), company (optional) and your area of interest.
  • Sponsorship enquiries: first name, last name, email, phone (optional), company name and your message.
  • Funding support enquiries: name, email, company, phone, business stage and a short description of your project.
  • FC Offers submissions: your business name, sector, offer details, website, and your name and email as the submitter.

We do not use tracking cookies or advertising pixels on this site.

How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and requests you send us.
  • To send you the First Connections newsletter and event updates, where you've asked for these.
  • To arrange sponsorship, funding-support discovery calls, or event bookings you've requested.
  • To review and publish business offers submitted for the FC Offers marketplace.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted service providers to run the site and community:

  • Email delivery and CRM platform: sends our newsletter and manages our contacts list for people who submit a form.
  • Cloud database provider: stores FC Offers marketplace listings.
  • Netlify (website hosting provider): hosts the website and processes form submissions via serverless functions.
  • Google Fonts (web font service): loads the typeface used across the site.
  • Eventbrite (external event booking platform): event booking pages linked from our Events page (that provider's own privacy policy applies once you leave our site).

We don't sell your personal data, and we only share it with the providers above as needed to run the services described.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and newsletter data for as long as you remain subscribed or as long as needed to respond to your enquiry, and no longer than necessary for the purpose it was collected for. You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see below).

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to ask us what personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to have it deleted, or to withdraw consent (for example, to unsubscribe from the newsletter). To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@first-connections.co.uk.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your data, contact us at hello@first-connections.co.uk.
